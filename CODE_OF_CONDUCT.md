# Code of conduct

## Purpose

This code of conduct sets out how participants treat each other, so that everyone can ask questions and propose changes without harassment.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful to every participant, including people who disagree with you.
- Keep comments on topic: the library, its documentation, and the change under discussion.
- Assume good faith, and ask for clarification before you conclude that a comment was meant to offend.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Do not harass other participants.
- Do not make personal attacks, insults, or demeaning comments.
- Do not publish another person's private information without their consent.

## Scope

This code applies to issues, pull requests, and other discussions about the project.

## Reporting

Report a violation by email to [massimodeluisa@me.com](mailto:massimodeluisa@me.com). Include links to the content involved.

## Enforcement

The maintainers may edit, lock, or remove content that breaks this code. They may also block people who repeat the behaviour.
